Recruitment / 团队招募

Graduate study

PhD Students

Peking University · Beijing · China

I am happy to hear from prospective doctoral students whose interests overlap with my research. Strong candidates typically have solid training in economics and econometrics, curiosity about policy-relevant questions, and the motivation to develop an independent research agenda.

How to express interest

Email a CV, transcripts, and a brief statement describing your research interests, preparation, and potential fit. You may also include a research paper or other writing sample. Formal admission is handled through the relevant Peking University doctoral admissions process.

Research position

Postdoctoral Researchers

Peking University · Beijing · China

I welcome inquiries from researchers completing, or recently completing, a PhD in economics or a related quantitative field. Applicants should demonstrate strong empirical skills, the ability to lead independent work, and an interest in collaborative research using administrative, survey, or spatial data.

Peking University Boya Postdoctoral Fellowship / 北京大学博雅博士后

Eligible applicants may also seek support through the two-year Peking University Boya Postdoctoral Fellowship. Under the university's 2026 call, the fellowship provides a basic annual salary of RMB 200,000 before tax, social insurance, occupational annuity, and housing-fund benefits, plus either a postdoctoral apartment or an annual RMB 60,000 housing allowance. Depending on the candidate, the host or department may provide an additional RMB 30,000, 60,000, or 90,000 per year.

申请人可同时关注北京大学博雅博士后项目。根据学校 2026 年申请公告，项目资助期为两年， 基本年薪为税前 20 万元，并提供保险、职业年金、公积金等福利，以及博士后公寓或每年 6 万元租房补贴；合作导师或院系可视申请人情况另提供每年 3 万、6 万或 9 万元配套资助。 具体条件与批次安排以北京大学当年官方公告为准。

How to apply

Email a CV, a research statement, one representative paper, and the names of two references. Please briefly describe the projects you hope to pursue and how they connect with the team's research. Appointments depend on available positions and the formal requirements of Peking University.

Year-round recruitment

Part-time Research Assistants

Based in Beijing or remote

Part-time RA opportunities are available throughout the year. Typical work may include literature reviews, data collection and cleaning, geospatial processing, econometric analysis, visualization, and replication. Applicants from economics, public policy, statistics, data science, or related fields are welcome.

What I look for

Careful and reliable work, clear communication, and proficiency in at least one of Stata, R, or Python are essential. Experience with GIS, machine learning, large datasets, or reproducible research workflows is especially useful.

How to apply

Email a CV, transcript, a short note on your interests, location, and weekly availability, plus a code sample, course project, or other example of research work. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis as project needs arise.

Applications and inquiries

Get in touch

Send materials to yhpan@pku.edu.cn with the subject line PhD Inquiry, Postdoc Inquiry, or Part-time RA Application. A short, specific email describing your fit is more helpful than a generic message.
