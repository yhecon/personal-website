Imagine that you can donate between ¥0 and ¥100 to a charitable project. Enter how much you would donate under each of the three disclosure settings below.

Scenario 1

Completely anonymous

No one will know whether you donated or how much you donated.

Scenario 2

Donors' names will be listed

If you donate, your name will be listed, but the amount will not be disclosed. If you do not donate, your name will not be listed.

Scenario 3

Names and amounts will be listed

If you donate, both your name and donation amount will be listed. If you do not donate, your name will not be listed.
